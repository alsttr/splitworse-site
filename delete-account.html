Account deletion

Delete your SplitWorse account

This page explains how to delete your SplitWorse account, what is deleted, and what is kept. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Delete it yourself, inside the app

  1. Open SplitWorse and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE when asked, to confirm. This is deliberate friction, because the action cannot be reversed.

Your account is removed straight away. You are signed out and cannot sign back in.

If you cannot reach the app

Email bluepigster@gmail.com from the address on the account, with the subject Delete my account. Your account will be deleted within 30 days, and sooner where possible. Requests from a different address cannot be accepted, because there would be no way to prove the account is yours.

What is deleted

DataWhen
Your sign-in credential, so the account can no longer be accessedImmediately
Your display name, username and email addressImmediately
Your profile photoImmediately
Your friendships and friend listImmediately
Your app preferences and settingsImmediately
Your name and email on any bug reports you sentImmediately, made anonymous

No additional retention period applies to the data above. It is removed from the live database at the moment you confirm. Routine encrypted backups are rotated on a rolling schedule and are used only to restore the service after a failure.

What is kept, and why

Expenses you shared with a group or a friend are kept. They are not only your records — they are part of everybody else's balances in that group. Deleting them would leave other people unable to work out who owes what.

In those shared records you appear as “Deleted user”. Your name, username, email and photo are gone; only the amounts and dates remain, so the ledger still adds up for the people you shared it with. These records are kept for as long as the group exists.

Your subscription

SplitWorse Pro is billed by Google Play or by Apple, not by SplitWorse, so deleting your account does not cancel it. Cancel the subscription first, in the Play Store or App Store under your subscriptions, or it will keep renewing.
