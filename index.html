Support

Something not adding up? Email bluepigster@gmail.com and include what you expected to happen. The in-app form is faster, because it attaches your app version and device automatically.

Reporting a bug from inside the app

Settings → Help → Report a problem. It fills in your app version, device and account so you do not have to.

Common questions

A balance looks wrong

SplitWorse reconciles every expense against its bill and refuses to round a difference away silently. If a figure looks off, send a bug report naming the group and the expense and it can be traced exactly.

I paid for Pro and it has not unlocked

Open the paywall and tap Restore purchases. Store purchases occasionally take a moment to confirm. If it still has not unlocked after a minute, email with the Apple ID email you purchased under.

How do I cancel Pro?

Pro is billed through your Apple ID or Google account, so it is cancelled there, not in SplitWorse. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period runs to its end.

What do the free limits cover?

Recording expenses, groups, friends, debt simplification, currency conversion and settling up are all free and unlimited. Gacha Your Bill and Trick or Treat give you two free rounds each. Pro makes those unlimited and adds Quick Split with history, and gamble statistics.

How do I redeem a code?

Paywall → Redeem a code. Codes work on iPhone and iPad only.

Deleting your account

Email bluepigster@gmail.com from the address on your account. Deletion is permanent and removes the records you own.
