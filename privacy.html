Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026

SplitWorse records what people spend together. That is inherently personal, so this policy says exactly what is stored, where it goes, and how to get rid of it. There is no advertising, no tracking across other apps, and no analytics SDK in this app.

What is collected

DataWhyWhen
Email address, display name, avatar imageTo create your account and let friends identify youWhen you sign up
Expenses, groups, friendships, settlements, receipt line items, gamble results This is the app's purpose — the shared record of who paid what As you use the app
Subscription statusTo unlock Pro features you have paid for If you subscribe
App version, operating system version, device model, device vendor identifier, your account IDSo a bug report can be reproduced Only when you submit a bug report
Preferences such as theme and default currencyTo remember your settings Stored on your device only

Not collected

No advertising identifier. No location. No contacts, photos or microphone access beyond images you deliberately pick for an avatar or a receipt. No third-party analytics. No data is sold, and none is shared with advertisers.

Who processes it

  • Supabase — hosts the database, authentication and avatar storage.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription status. It receives an app user ID and the purchase receipt Apple or Google issues.
  • Apple or Google — processes the payment and, if you use Sign in with Apple or Google, the sign-in itself. If you choose Apple's private relay, we never see your real email address.

Nobody else receives your data.

What other people can see

An expense is shared by design. Anyone in a group with you can see the expenses in that group, who paid, and the resulting balances. A friend you share a one-to-one expense with can see that expense. Your email address is not shown to other users; your display name and avatar are.

How long it is kept

Your data is kept while your account exists. Delete your account and the records you own are deleted with it. Expenses in a shared group are part of other people's records too, so your personal details are removed from them rather than the group's history being rewritten for everyone else.

Deleting your account

Open Settings in the app and choose Delete account. It takes effect immediately and cannot be undone: your sign-in, your profile and your friendships are deleted outright.

Expenses you shared with a group stay in that group. They are part of everyone else’s balances as much as yours, so removing them would leave other people unable to reconcile what they are owed. Your name is replaced with “Deleted user” in those records.

If you would rather someone did it for you, email bluepigster@gmail.com from the address on the account.

Children

SplitWorse is not directed at children and should not be used by anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the effective date above changes and the new version is published here before it takes effect.
